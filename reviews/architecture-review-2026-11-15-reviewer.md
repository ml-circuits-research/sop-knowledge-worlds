## Serious errors

1. **The test runner reports a killed file as passing.** `tools/test-run.mjs:144-156` retries a killed file 5 times. After the loop it logs `FAIL` only when `r.fail || r.code` is set. A run killed by a signal has `code === null` and `fail === 0`, so it is logged `ok`, and a file with 0 tests also passes. In this run, `tests/derived.test.mjs` (4 tests, which check that the derived shards are fresh) was killed 5 times and then logged `ok 89.5 s 2648 MB 0 pass` (log in /tmp/review-know-tests.log). *Why it matters:* the main freshness test of the derived shards never ran, yet the round was green. *Fix:* treat `r.killed` or `r.tests === 0` as FAIL in the verdict and in `bad`, and print "not run" in the summary.

2. **A CC BY-SA 3.0 dictionary is published without a catalog or licence entry.** `docs/worlds/w.lang.ro/dictionary-unimorph/*.tsv` (23 files; header `# license: CC-BY-SA-3.0`, source `unimorph-ron`) is published under docs/. The w.lang.ro entry in `docs/worlds/index.json` lists `licenses ['CC0-1.0']` and `sources ['hand-core-1']` only. `docs/worlds/LICENSES.md` has no unimorph section, because `tools/build-catalog.mjs:66-90` reads only `.sop` shards. *Why it matters:* the share-alike terms and the attribution are missing, and a host cannot filter the share-alike part out. *Fix:* catalog the TSV folders as shards with their own licence (or convert them to SOP shards), and add a test that every published file has a header listed in index.json.

3. **The time-of-day values cannot join k.time.** `k.scenes/schema.sop:38` (`k.scenes.scene.time`) and `k.attributes/schema.sop:106` (`k.attributes.usage.time`) use `enum=morning enum=day enum=evening enum=night`. k.time's parts of the day are morning, afternoon, evening and night (`k.time/shards/clock.sop:7-10`), and `k.time.hour.part` (`k.time/schema.sop:126`) uses afternoon. JOINS-REPORT shows 3 of 4 values meeting (`k.kinds/JOINS-REPORT.md`, the rows for `k.scenes.scene.time` and `k.attributes.usage.time`). *Why it matters:* "things used in the afternoon" and "a day scene" give wrong or empty answers. *Fix:* declare `type=k.time.part_of_day by=name` on both slots and replace `day` (add `day` as a span in k.time if it is needed).

4. **Capitals are stored in both directions, and the copies already disagree.** `k.geo.country.capital` and `k.geo.city.capital_of` both hold the same 263 pairs (for example `k.geo/shards/africa-borders.sop:10` and `:11`). Two further `capital_of` facts have no counterpart: Caribbean Netherlands has `capital=Kralendijk` (`k.geo/shards/psys2-countries.sop:39`), but Oranjestad and The Bottom are also `capital_of="Caribbean Netherlands"` (`k.geo/shards/psys2-cities-north-america.sop:10-11` and `north-america-borders.sop:20-21`). *Why it matters:* "one home per fact" is broken, and "what is the capital of X" depends on which wire a host reads. *Fix:* keep `k.geo.country.capital` as the home and derive `capital_of` by a rule in k.geo (written into `derived` like the other rule outputs). Oranjestad and The Bottom are the seats of Sint Eustatius and Saba, which needs a `seat_of` key, not `capital_of`.

5. **Size is stored twice, and the two disagree.** `k.scenes.thing.size` (5 classes, `k.scenes/schema.sop:23`) and `k.attributes.number.size_about` (metres, `k.attributes/schema.sop:87`) are both labelled by a model for the same things: 623 things have both, and 8 differ by two classes or more. Examples: fence is `medium` (`k.scenes/shards/things-f.sop:21`) but 20 m (`k.attributes/shards/numbers-a-f.sop:325`); chime is `large` (`things-c.sop:150`) but 0.5 m (`numbers-a-f.sop:203`); rope is `small` but 10 m. *Why it matters:* two answers to "how big is it", and drawings scaled from the class contradict the number. *Fix:* make `size_about` the home and compute the class by rule from it, as `weight_class` and `speed_class` already are (`k.attributes/schema.sop:78-80`). Keep a told class only where no number exists.

6. **Thing facts are split between k.attributes and k.commonsense, under different key names.** `k.attributes.thing` has `material`, `can` and `used_for` (`k.attributes/schema.sop:59-62`). `k.commonsense.thing` has `made_of`, `can` and `used_for` (`k.commonsense/schema.sop:13-15`). 80 things are in both: 38 have `can` in both and 9 have material/made_of in both. Car is `material=metal can=move used_for=travelling` (`k.attributes/shards/things-big.sop:21`) and also `made_of=metal can=drive used_for=travel` (`k.commonsense/shards/core.sop:13`); boat appears at `things-big.sop:16` and `core.sop:14`. COVERAGE.md lists both homes for each attribute (`docs/worlds/COVERAGE.md`, the material, can and used-for rows). *Why it matters:* the values drift (move or drive, travelling or travel), and every reader has to union two types. *Fix:* make k.attributes.thing the home of material, can and used_for, and type `used_for` as a join to `k.actions.action`. Keep only `at`, `usually_count` and the rules in k.commonsense, and have the rules read k.attributes.

7. **The licence policy still allows NC, and nothing enforces a licence list.** `AGENTS.md:34-35` ("CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA") and `PLAN.md:81-87` allow non-commercial sources, and the generated `LICENSES.md` repeats "non-commercial stays non-commercial" (`tools/build-catalog.mjs:76-77`). `tools/validate.mjs:62` checks only that a `# license:` line exists. None of the 1,154 headers is NC, ND, research-only or unknown today; the licences found are CC0-1.0 (383), WordNet-3.0 (376), CC-BY-SA-4.0 (197), Apache-2.0 AND Unicode-3.0 (126), CC-BY-4.0 (52), public-domain (10), ODbL-1.0 (8), Unicode-3.0 (1) and MIT (1). *Why it matters:* the next worker who follows AGENTS.md may import NC data, and the validator would accept it. *Fix:* rewrite both documents to the house rule (CC-BY-SA and CDLA-Sharing only in their own shards; never NC, ND, research-only or unknown). Add an allowlist of SPDX ids to validate.mjs, and a check that a share-alike licence never shares a shard with another source.

## Structural

8. **Colours have two homes, and the basic-colour enum is copied 6 times.** All 33 `k.colours.colour` names are also `k.values.colour` facts (171 names). `k.values.colour.basic` (`k.values/schema.sop:20`) and `k.colours.colour.family` (`k.colours/schema.sop:20`) are the same idea under two keys. The 11-value enum is repeated in `k.values/schema.sop:20,28`, `k.drawing/schema.sop:22`, `k.attributes/schema.sop:58,71` and `k.colours/schema.sop:20`, and a test keeps the copies equal (`tests/shared-modules.test.mjs:31-43`). *Why it matters:* a new basic colour needs six edits, and the test is there only because of the copies. *Fix:* make k.colours the one home for colour names, with rgb, family and a `basic=true` subset. Replace every enum with a declared join `type=k.colours.colour by=name`, and drop `k.values.colour`.

9. **Shapes have four vocabularies.** `k.values.shape` (33 names; `k.values/shards/shapes.sop`) and `k.shapes.shape` (39 names; 30 shared; trapezoid, curve and dot only in k.values) overlap. Two more lists are enums: `k.drawing.part.shape` (`k.drawing/schema.sop:16`) and `k.diagrams.shape.shape` / `w.diagram.node.shape` (`k.diagrams/schema.sop:41`, `w.diagram/schema.sop:33`, using oval and ellipse, diamond for k.shapes' rhombus). *Why it matters:* "a diamond" means three things depending on the world. *Fix:* make k.shapes the home, with the drawing primitives as a `primitive=true` subset. Join the drawing, diagram and node shape slots to `k.shapes.shape`, and record ellipse/oval and diamond/rhombus as words.

10. **Verb forms have three homes plus a separate rule in JS.** "went" is at `w.lang.en/shards/irregular-forms.sop:149`, `k.words/shards/go.sop:13` and `k.actions/shards/actions-move.sop:61`. `tools/actions-core.mjs:17-24` writes the third-person, past and -ing forms of k.actions with its own regular expressions, apart from `w.lang.en.inflect`/`w.lang.en.class` and the library's `inflectBy`. *Why it matters:* the forms can disagree (`k.actions` lists "going", `w.lang.en` generates it), and English morphology leaks into two neutral worlds. *Fix:* make w.lang.en (irregular_form plus the class dictionary) the one home. Drop the form core.words from k.actions and k.words, and have hosts call `inflectBy`.

11. **Number words are in k.values and in w.lang.en, and the compounds are listed rather than generated.** All 13 `w.lang.en.numeral` facts and 10 of the 11 `w.lang.en.ordinal` facts repeat `k.values.number` facts: three is at `k.values/shards/numbers.sop:11` and `w.lang.en/shards/numbers.sop:20`. k.values also lists every compound up to 100 as a fact ("twenty-three" at `numbers.sop:51`). Comparatives (`k.values/schema.sop:43`) and set words (`:76`) are English lexicon too. *Why it matters:* there are two homes, and an English word-formation rule is stored as 100 rows in a neutral world. *Fix:* move number words, comparatives and set words to w.lang.en, with a composition rule (tens plus units) for the library. Keep in k.values only the neutral concepts (scales with steps, directions, positions).

12. **`is_a` lives in three places, and the k.kinds.parent copies are mislabelled.** `k.kinds.kind.is_a` (`k.kinds/schema.sop:17`), `k.words.sense.is_a/kind_of` (`k.words/schema.sop:28,33`) and the 13,699 `k.kinds.parent` copies all carry it. For square, see `k.shapes/shards/shapes.sop:10`, `k.words/shards/sq.sop:12` and `k.kinds/shards/wordnet-2.sop:490-491`. `k.kinds.parent` stores part, use, material and origin edges under the key `is_a` (`k.kinds/shards/part_of.sop`: `name=ankle is_a=leg dimension=part_of`). These copies have no `derived=true` and carry `core.source="wordnet 3.1"` or `"hand-core 1"` instead of `rule …`, against the convention in `README.md:21-23`. *Why it matters:* `is_a` means "part of" in one world, the copies go stale when k.parts or k.body change, and tools cannot tell them from told facts. *Fix:* rename the key to `parent` (or `holonym` for part_of), mark the copies `derived=true` with the generator as their source, or compute the graph in the library from the declared joins (Q59).

13. **Application wording sits in knowledge worlds.** `k.feelings.feeling.reply` (`k.feelings/schema.sop:29`) holds reply texts such as "I am glad you are happy! What made your day so good?" (`k.feelings/shards/feelings.sop:6`). Ten deny rules carry English `why=` sentences (`k.commonsense/schema.sop:35,42,49,57,102`, `k.animals/schema.sop:53`, `k.shapes/schema.sop:34,36`, `k.scenes/schema.sop:28`). There are 95 free-text prose slots in k.* (about, result, example, caused_by…). *Why it matters:* README.md:74-75 says the wording of replies stays in the application, and none of this can be said in Romanian. *Fix:* move `reply` and the `why` messages to w.lang.en templates keyed by the feeling or rule id (`{thing}` slots). Keep prose in k.* only as documentation, and mark it as such.

14. **The English lexicon is spread over 40 worlds, and AGENTS.md rule 2 contradicts the language world.** There are 6,998 `core.word` wires: k.words 3,007, k.objects 517, k.geo 498, k.music 394, k.animals 316, k.values 287, and so on, against 17 in w.lang.en. k.actions also holds 316 English verbs and the role markers; 43 of its 44 markers are also `w.lang.en.prep` facts. `AGENTS.md:16-17` says "English only inside worlds … Translation … never here", while w.lang.ro is published here. *Why it matters:* a second language has to restate or shadow 40 worlds, and the house rule ("English wording … in w.lang.en") is not what agents read. *Fix:* rewrite AGENTS rule 2. Keep identity names as neutral ids and move `core.word`, verb lists and markers into w.lang.en lexicon facts keyed by type or slot id, so that w.lang.ro can mirror them. The k.words lemmas can stay as the shared dictionary.

15. **Library mechanisms are re-implemented in tools.** `tools/lib/lang-en.mjs` has `inflect` (l.15-33), `frameReader` (a 120-line parser, l.112-230), `articleFor` (l.234-239) and `realiseEn` (l.244-258). The pinned library exports `inflectBy`, `articleFor`, `realise`, `parseText`, `realiseFrame` and `patternReader` (`tools/sopworld/sopworld.mjs` export list). `tools/lib/choose.mjs:41` defines `solveChoices`, which the library also exports. These are imported by 9 tests and 3 tools (w.lang.en.*, w.lang.ro.frames, k.compose, lang-en-core, lang-en-diagram-frames, lib/lang-ro.mjs). English rules are hard-coded in JS: the consonant regex (`lang-en.mjs:11`), a naive `${x}s` plural (`:132`) and a `/^[aeiou]/` article fallback (`:237`). *Why it matters:* tests check the local copy, not the library a host runs, and the two can diverge silently. *Fix:* point the tests at the library functions, delete the local copies, and file any missing behaviour as a library request.

16. **Knowledge is held in JavaScript, not in the shards.**
    - `tools/joins.mjs:24-40` decides the sense of a drawing from its shard file name (`shardSense`, regular expressions on `animals`, `food`…), and from a `KIND_SENSE` table and a `TYPES` table.
    - `tools/key-words.mjs:17-38` has a hand table `EXTRA` of query words.
    - `tools/lang-en-morph.mjs:9-10` holds `IRREGULAR_VERBS` and `IRREGULAR_ADJECTIVES` written in the tool.
    - `tools/actions-data.mjs`, `scenes-data.mjs` (with the notation `"name[=drawing], count, where[, side][, @on][, *]"`, l.3-4), `objects-hand-data.mjs` (1,010 lines), `colours-shapes-core.mjs`, `people-core.mjs`, `values-core.mjs` and `acts-core.mjs` hold the hand-written facts as arrays with comma-joined lists. Their shards are outputs.
    - The reasons for leaving joins undeclared are a JS table (`tools/joins-report.mjs:27`).

    *Why it matters:* this breaks AGENTS rules 1 and 3 (SOP only, no notation in values). A hand edit to a shard is overwritten at the next run, and the sense of a fact changes when a file is renamed. *Fix:* make the SOP shard the hand-written home and turn these tools into checkers. Declare the sense of each type as a schema fact (for example `sense=animal` on the type) and drop the file-name rule.

17. **Romanian sits outside w.lang.ro.** `tools/lang-ro-core.mjs` (173 lines with Romanian: classes, endings, examples such as "câine, câini" at l.34-38), `tools/lang-ro-frames.mjs` (49 lines), `tools/lang-ro-unimorph.mjs` (3) and `docs/worlds/k.words/WORLD.md:34-35` (cursiv, îngroșat, nisipiu). The k.words senses picked for Romanian lemmas are referenced by wire id from `w.lang.ro/dictionary/*.tsv`. *Why it matters:* the "no Romanian outside w.lang.ro" rule is broken in the tools, and the Romanian data's real home is a JS file. *Fix:* the same as finding 16: the w.lang.ro shards become the home, and the tools only check them. Remove the Romanian examples from k.words/WORLD.md and point to w.lang.ro instead.

18. **Joins: 114 undeclared, several clearly joins, and free text that should be typed.** `k.kinds/JOINS-REPORT.md:216-336` lists 114 keys whose values name things of another type. Many meet 100 %: `k.animals.animal.class`→`k.animals.class` (13/13), `k.scenes.has.on` (43/43), `k.procedures.method.act` and `step.act`→`k.actions.act`, `k.time.month.season_north/south`→`k.time.season`, and the colour, shape and direction slots of k.drawing, k.objects and k.faces.
    - Free text that should be typed: `k.animals.animal.eats` has 89 values and only 7 match k.food ("insects", "mice", "seeds"; `k.animals/schema.sop:34`). `k.attributes.thing.used_for` is `kind=string` (`k.attributes/schema.sop:62`). `k.geo.country.language` (`k.geo/schema.sop:24`) points to no world.
    - The colour slots of the world schemas are free atoms: `w.diagram/schema.sop:39`, `w.chart/schema.sop:27`, `w.face/schema.sop:42`, `w.map/schema.sop:23,31`. `w.chart.chart.unit` (`w.chart/schema.sop:20`) should join `k.units.unit`.
    - `w.map.place.name`, `w.map.pin.on` and `w.map.view.centre` (`w.map/schema.sop:20,28,41`) should join k.geo. `w.document.sentence.fact` (`w.document/schema.sop:75`) and the `sense` column of `w.lang.ro/dictionary/*.tsv` hold wire ids that the engine never checks.
    - `k.story.obstacle.source` (`k.story/schema.sop:74`) lacks the `type=k.story.source by=name` that its 5 siblings carry.
    - `k.animals.animal.thing` is declared `type=k.objects.object` (`k.animals/schema.sop:39`), while its comment (l.21-24) says it names a `k.scenes.thing`.

    *Why it matters:* undeclared joins are invisible to the engine, the samplers and `k.kinds.relation`. *Fix:* declare every join that meets 100 % now, type the free-text slots, and write a reason for each one left open.

19. **The naming key and the language feature keys differ from world to world.** The identity key is `name` in 114 types, `text` in k.actions.verb, k.values.comparative, k.values.number and 26 w.lang.en types, `form` in 5 w.lang.ro types (det, pron, prep, numeral, particle), and `word` in k.words. The two language worlds name the same features differently: `w.lang.en.det.definite`/`number` against `w.lang.ro.det.def`/`num` (`w.lang.en/schema.sop:21-22`, `w.lang.ro/schema.sop:80-82`). Booleans are written as `enum=yes enum=no` in `w.lang.en.det.definite`, `k.attributes.narration.narrate` (`k.attributes/schema.sop:194`) and `k.diagrams.kind.groups`, but as `kind=boolean` elsewhere. Descriptions use `about` (55 types), `gloss`, `note`, `why`, `title` or `label`. *Why it matters:* every reader needs a table per world, and the two languages cannot share one reader without key maps. *Proposed convention:* `name` for the identity, `text` only for surface strings inside w.lang.*, `about` for prose, `kind=boolean` for two-valued keys, and the feature names of a shared `w.lang` base module (`number`, `gender`, `case`, `person`, `definite`) that w.lang.en and w.lang.ro both extend. **Worlds that break it:** k.actions, k.values, k.words, w.lang.en, w.lang.ro, k.attributes, k.diagrams.

20. **Facts about slots are spread over six types in three worlds, written two ways.** `k.units.measure` writes `world=k.geo type=country key=population` with a short type (`k.units/shards/measures.sop:6`). `k.attributes.found/shows/range/narration/typical`, `k.kinds.relation` and `k.diagrams.fill` write full type ids (`type=k.geo.country`). *Why it matters:* there are six homes for metadata about one slot, and a join on (type, key) fails between them. *Fix:* one convention: a full type id plus a key, with a declared join to `core.type`. Better still, put unit, salience and narration on the `core.slot` itself, or in one `k.kinds.slot` fact.

21. **Quantity shapes are inconsistent.** k.space puts units in key names (`orbit_years`, `orbit_days_about`, `day_hours_about`, `age_years_about`, `light_years_about`; `k.space/schema.sop:25,35-41`), and has orbit in years exact and days approximate on one type. Elsewhere the unit is in `k.units.measure` (`size_about`, `weight_about`). Ranges are `low`/`high` (`k.attributes/schema.sop:166-167`), `min`/`max` (k.genres.part and step, k.story.element, k.motion.letters) or `onset_min`/`onset_max`… (k.motion.step). k.people `height_about` is a percentage of a grown man. *Fix:* no units in key names, the unit only in k.units.measure, and ranges as `low`/`high`. **Worlds that break it:** k.space, k.genres, k.story, k.motion, k.people.

22. **There are four bibliographies, and six works are cited in more than one.** `k.genres.source`, `k.story.source`, `k.interest.source` and `k.diagrams.source` are the same type (name, cite, year). Aristotle, Freytag 1863, Labov & Waletzky 1967, Mandler & Johnson 1977 and Stein & Glenn 1979 are in both `k.genres/shards/sources.sop` (l.6-10) and `k.story/shards/sources.sop` (l.6-15), and Cowan 2001 is in both k.genres and k.interest. `k.attributes.range.cite` (`k.attributes/schema.sop:171`) is a free string. Ids are inconsistent too (labov1972 against labovWaletzky1967). *Fix:* one `k.sources` world (name, cite, year, doi), with declared joins from every `source` and `cite` key.

23. **Enums are restated instead of joined.**
    - The 12 senses appear at `k.attributes/schema.sop:86,104,118` and `k.kinds/schema.sop:39`, plus `SENSES` in `tools/joins.mjs:22`.
    - Season, weather and time-of-day appear in k.scenes (`k.scenes/schema.sop:38-40`) and k.attributes (`k.attributes/schema.sop:105-107`).
    - Age appears in k.attributes (`:108`), k.people (`k.people/schema.sop:18`) and k.faces (`k.faces/schema.sop:39`, which lacks baby).
    - `k.diagrams.kind.form/layout/role` and `k.diagrams.shape.shape` (`k.diagrams/schema.sop:25,27,30,41`) copy `w.diagram/schema.sop:19,20,32,33`.

    *Fix:* one owning type per value set (k.time.season, a new k.weather, k.people.age, k.kinds.sense, w.diagram's enums), with joins everywhere else.

24. **Shards named after the application split entities across sources.** 19 `psys2*` shards sit in 10 worlds (10 of them in k.geo), against `AGENTS.md:10` ("no PSys2 names … into the worlds"). 234 of the 253 countries are spread over 3 shards (continent, `*-borders`, `psys2-countries`), 70 animals over 2-3 shards, and 48 cities appear twice (for example Kralendijk at `psys2-cities-north-america.sop:9` and `north-america-borders.sop:19`). *Why it matters:* AGENTS rule 5 (one shard answers one kind of question) is broken, since "the capital of X" needs 3 shards, and the application's name sits in a reusable catalog. *Fix:* merge by natural key (continent, letter). `core.source` per wire already keeps the provenance, so shards need to be split only by licence.

25. **Cross-world identity is the English lemma, and its home is the wrong world.** `k.attributes.same` (3,581 facts; `k.attributes/schema.sop:116-122`) resolves "one name, one thing" across worlds. It exists because every world keys its things by an English string (bear the animal and Bear the river: `docs/worlds/JOINS.md`), and it lives in k.attributes, which is about attributes. *Fix:* move identity into k.kinds, or into a small `k.things` world with neutral ids (for example a WordNet synset or another stable id), and have the worlds refer to those ids.
