# w.room: rooms and seating capacity

A reusable world pattern, source hand-core 1, CC0-1.0. A room has an explicit
name and optional nonnegative integer seats: how many people its stated seating
capacity accommodates. It is a count, not floor area, current occupancy, free
space or availability. No count default or typical capacity is supplied.

The enclosed physical-place sense is already identified by published
k.words.room.noun.1 (WordNet 3.1); that source wire is cited as lexical evidence,
not copied or relicensed. k.words.room.noun.2 and noun.3 are other senses and are
not room-instance aliases. Room names need not be globally unique; wire identity
is separate. Missing capacity is unknown. Existing w.seating.table.seats applies
to a table; it cannot turn a room into a table or prove room capacity.

Rooms are independent of calendars. No booking, time availability, quietness,
relative size, occupancy, capacity conversion or native binding is claimed.
Generic std.acts operate only on the explicit room records.
