# w.lang.ro: Romanian as a language world (first draft)

A language world (block "09:25"; reviewer 09:35, 09:45, 10:20, 11:20): what is known about Romanian, so that an
application reads Romanian requests into the same acts and roles as English and answers in Romanian. Romanian words
are its data (forms, endings, lemmas); its types, keys and comments are English. It joins the domain worlds only by
sense ids (a lemma of the dictionary -> a `k.words` sense or a `k.actions` verb) and by relation names (a preposition's
`rel` is one of `w.lang.en`'s, its `layout` the `k.layouts` relation w.lang.en gives the same `rel`); no Romanian word
is in any other world. Everything is written by hand in this repository (`hand-core 1`, CC0-1.0): no dictionary,
treebank or word list was imported.

## Compact rules plus an external dictionary

| part | what | size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shards/morphology.sop` | 45 paradigm classes (23 noun, adjective and article, 22 verb) with inheritance, 341 cells (features -> suffix with the stem's ordered alternations), the vowels, 14 spelling folds, the clitic joiner | 402 wires, 60.1 kB |
| `shards/irregular.sop` | the forms no class covers: fi, avea (with its conditional and perfect auxiliaries), vrea, da, lua, veni, vedea, împărți; the imperatives fă, refă, du, mergi, treci; speli, numeri, umplut; the plurals of zi, ou, om, pește, cal, copil, băiat, femeie, pasăre, fereastră, zăpadă; nou; the indefinite article un, o, niște, unui, unei, unor | 225 wires, 33.3 kB |
| `shards/closed.sop` | 69 determiners and articles, 65 pronouns (free, bound and joined clitics), 30 prepositions as relations, 32 numerals with value and gender, 44 conjunctions, adverbs, particles and interjections | 240 wires, 32.3 kB |
| `shards/templates.sop` | 3 reply templates (make, remove, change) with agreement, the article included ("am desenat o casă roșie", "mașina este acum galbenă") | 3 wires |
| `dictionary/<letter>.tsv` | 256 lemmas: `lemma<TAB>class<TAB>gender<TAB>sense`, sorted by folded lemma, one file per first folded letter (21 files), a header with source and licence | 15.0 kB (9.2 kB of rows, 36 bytes a lemma) |

The world is 949 wires (with the schema's 79), 140 kB; loading and compiling it (`morphOf`) takes
about 80 ms. No form is listed: every form of a lemma is generated by its class (2,916 cells for the 256 lemmas).

Paradigm classes (`w.lang.ro.class`), by a name that says the gender or conjugation and the ending:
nouns `m.cons` (copac), `m.e` (câine) > `m.e.oa` (soare, sori), `n.e` (desen) > `n.e.oa` (picior, picioare),
`n.e.ă` (măr, mere), `n.uri` (cerc) > `n.u` (titlu), `f.ă` (casă) > `f.ă.e` (pară, pere), `f.ăi` (mașină) >
`f.ăi.a` (barcă, bărci; hartă, hărți), `f.e` (pâine) > `f.e.alt` (carte, cărți; floare, flori; noapte, nopți),
`f.ie` (linie), `f.ea` (stea); adjectives `a.1` (galben) > `a.1i` (mic), `a.1.oa` (frumos, frumoasă), `a.e` (verde,
verzi), `a.u` (roșu, roșie), `a.iu` (portocaliu), `a.ru` (albastru, albaștri; negru, neagră), `a.inv` (mov) >
`det.art` (the indefinite article un: every form irregular, agreeing with its noun as an adjective does); verbs
`v.a` (muta) > `v.a.ă3` (arăta), `v.a.ă` (adăuga), `v.a.u` (juca), `v.a.oa` (înota), `v.a.ez` (desena) > `v.a.iez`
(copia), `v.ea` > `v.ea.u` (putea), `v.ea.ă` (plăcea), `v.e` (face, începe) > `v.e.n` (obține) > `v.e.s.n` (pune),
`v.e.s.d` (deschide), `v.e.s.g` (șterge), `v.e.s.t` (scoate), `v.e.s.c` (întoarce), `v.e.iu` (scrie), `v.i.esc`
(vorbi) > `v.i.iesc` (înlocui).

Features (the cells' keys, for the library: questions.md Q53): `num` sg pl; `case` nomacc gendat (pronouns: nom acc
gendat); `def` yes no; `gender` m f n; `person` 1 2 3; `tense` inf imper pres subj part, and cond perf for the
auxiliary avea. A noun has 8 cells (num x case x def), an adjective 12 (gender x num x case), a verb 16 (infinitive,
imperative singular and plural, present and conjunctive by person and number, participle).

## How a host reads it

```js
import { World, morphOf, dictionary, analyse, generate, fold, splitClitics, realise, templateFor } from 'sopworld';
const m = morphOf(world, { types: { cls: 'w.lang.ro.class', ending: 'w.lang.ro.ending', irregular: 'w.lang.ro.irregular',
  fold: 'w.lang.ro.fold', split: 'w.lang.ro.split', template: 'w.lang.ro.template', vowel: 'w.lang.ro.vowel' } });
const d = dictionary({ read: (letter) => fetchText(`w.lang.ro/dictionary/${letter}.tsv`), fold: (s) => fold(m, s) });
splitClitics(m, 'desenează-mi');                      // ['desenează', 'mi']: mi is a joined clitic of w.lang.ro.pron
analyse(m, 'desenează', { dictionary: d });           // desena, v.a.ez, tense=imper person=2 num=sg (and pres 3sg)
generate(m, 'carte', 'f.e.alt', { num: 'pl', case: 'nomacc', def: 'no' });   // cărți
realise(m, templateFor(m, 'make'), { kind: 'casă', colour: 'roșu' }, { dictionary: d, base: { num: 'sg', case: 'nomacc' } }); // am desenat casă roșie
```

A Romanian session loads the schema and the four shards (138 kB, once) and, for each word, the dictionary file of its
first folded letter: on PSys2's commands, 4.6 files (3.8 kB) a message. The closed classes are read by form (folded
for a word typed without diacritics).

## Measured (tests/w.lang.ro.test.mjs)

- Round trip analyse(generate(x)) in exact spelling: 2,916 of the 2,916 cells of the 256 lemmas (every class and cell
  with the first lemma of the class: 556 of 556), with no exception since sopworld 0.63 (Q52: an alternation is undone
  only where the stem that regenerates the form needs it: uriași, englezi, îngroși come back).
- Round trip through the folds (a form typed without diacritics): 2,916 of 2,916 (was 2,249 before sopworld 0.63
  indexed folded suffixes, Q51).
- Coverage on PSys2's Romanian commands (art.draw, docs.editor, ui.shell, data.charts: 240 messages, 1,191 words
  after the clitics are split; 18 names, letters and foreign words not counted; gold lemmas by hand,
  `sources/psys2-eval-ro`): 1,185 (99.5%) have their lemma among the readings in exact spelling, 99.2% as the first
  reading. Through the folds 99.7% (1,187), and the casual variants typed without diacritics 99.7% (1,163 of 1,167;
  92.2% before sopworld 0.63). Missed in exact spelling: kiwi, reprezintă, trebui (not in the dictionary) and spânzuratoarea (written
  without its ă in the source).
- Every lemma has a sense: the 9 of the first draft without one (cursiv, orizontal, vertical, îngroșat, numerotat,
  nisipiu, anterior, gros, îngroșa) are joined to the English senses k.words added for them in block "16:10"
  (italic, horizontal, vertical, bold, numbered, sandy, anterior, thick adjectives; thicken verb).

## Not yet

Syntax facts (word order of requests and questions, negation, clitic doubling, partitives), the argument frames of
the acts in Romanian, and a reading into acts are the next draft (block "09:25" items 2, 3, 5). Imperative cells of
verbs that have no imperative (vrea, putea) are what the class gives, not words. The indefinite article in a template
is a role `art` that the host fills with the lemma `un` (`{art@kind}` agrees with the noun: o casă, un copac); the
definite article is a cell of the noun (`{thing:def=yes}`), as the library answered (its Q40).

## A bigger dictionary, derived from UniMorph (blocks "18:20" item 2, "19:35")

`dictionary-unimorph/<letter>.tsv` (23 files, 3,466 lemmas, 67.7 kB, **CC BY-SA 3.0**, a folder of its own):
lemma, class, gender, an empty sense, in the hand dictionary's format. `tools/lang-ro-unimorph.mjs` reads UniMorph
Romanian (84,679 rows, 4,388 lemma and part-of-speech pairs; `sources/unimorph-ron`) and puts each lemma into the
paradigm class whose generated forms contain every form UniMorph gives for it (at least 3), by sets of forms, not by
UniMorph's labels (the file labels some noun cells wrongly: casa as plural). Compared: nouns without the vocative and
with the article words stripped; adjectives' indefinite forms; verbs' present, conjunctive, positive imperative,
infinitive and participle (the past tenses and the gerund are not cells here). Where several classes generate all
the forms, the one with the fewest forms UniMorph lacks wins, then the first class.

- A class fits 3,612 of 4,388 (82%; nouns 2,012 of 2,308, verbs 951 of 1,205, adjectives 649 of 875); in 1,339 of
  them another class generates the same forms. No class for 753 (alternations no class has yet: sc > șt in gâscă,
  gâște; ea > e; irregulars such as greu, grea; and some noise of the file); 23 have fewer than 3 forms.
- Of the 156 UniMorph lemmas that the hand dictionary has, the hand class is among the fitting ones for 144 (92%).
  The hand rows win; their lemmas are not repeated in the derived files.
- A host loads both: for a letter, the hand file, then the derived one (`dictionary({ read })` over the two folders).
  The derived rows have no sense: they read a word to its lemma and class, not to a meaning.
- Coverage on PSys2's ro-en-meaning set (10,259 rows at psys2 a4357c2e, read in place, `node tools/lang-ro-coverage.mjs
  <psys2>/training/eval/ro-en-meaning/rows.jsonl`; a word counts when some reading exists: a closed-class word or a
  form a lemma's class generates): hand Romanian 28,752 words, 75.6% read with the hand dictionary, 81.7% with
  UniMorph added; machine Romanian 17,855 words, 51.8% and 59.8%. The meaning of a row needs the frames layer
  (block "18:20" item 1), not yet written.

## Frames and order (block "18:20" item 1)

`shards/frames.sop` (`tools/lang-ro-frames.mjs`, hand-written, CC0): 39 frames from Romanian requests to the acts of
k.actions with their roles, in w.lang.en's frame vocabulary (`w.lang.ro.frame` name, act, mode, set, priority, needs,
role, example; `w.lang.ro.part` frame, at, is, act, role, takes, lexicon, max_words, word, phrase, optional), each naming
the English frame it says (`en`: same act, mode, needs; its roles among the English frame's roles), so one reader reads
both: `tools/lib/lang-ro.mjs` gives w.lang.en's reference reader the Romanian words. Verbs: every form the class of a
dictionary lemma generates (434 forms), for the lemmas whose sense reaches the act (a k.actions verb, or a k.words verb
sense whose English word is a k.actions verb of the act: scoate -> remove, alege -> choose -> pick). Prepositions are
w.lang.ro.prep (relation names of w.lang.en), so a role marked by one (`takes=prep`) is the place with its marker.
Phrase groups: leads (te rog, poți să, vreau să, hai să...), the politeness words a reader skips anywhere ("..., te
rog"), the clitics joined to an imperative (șterge-l, scoate-ți) and the bound ones (îl, o), wishes (vreau, aș vrea);
lexicons: comparatives (mai mare / mai mic and their agreeing forms), directions (la stânga, în sus). Order facts
(`w.lang.ro.syntax`, 6): an adjective follows its noun (the reader's kind phrase: the first noun is the head, the
adjectives after it its values: "Desenează o casă roșie" -> kind casă, value roșie), the genitive possessor without a
preposition (culoarea casei), the article as a suffix, joined and bound clitics, the conjunctive after a wish. A
Romanian frame takes its English frame's fixed arguments and frame role maps ("Numește-te Pixel": attribute name).
Measured on PSys2's ro-en-meaning set (read in place; a request counts when its reference reading is one request of a
frame act): hand Romanian 2,294 requests, read by a frame 69.3 %, the same act and mode 54.3 %, the same act and roles
39.9 %; machine Romanian 3,597 requests, 41.3 / 28.3 / 25.3 % (`tests/w.lang.ro.frames.test.mjs`; roles compared in the
roles of k.actions through w.lang.en's role maps). Not yet: verbs with no sense in the dictionaries (conecta, încărca
are words of their frames meanwhile), fragments without a verb ("Ochi fericiți, te rog"), arithmetic and the questions of
other worlds (talk, maps), multi-request sentences.
