# k.objects: common things without a drawing

Generated by `tools/coverage.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

The list of common things: the 1,500 most frequent English words (frequency-words 2018, film and television
subtitles) that are a noun in WordNet 3.1, are mainly used as a noun, and name a physical thing: a hyponym of physical
object, living thing, food or artifact in their first noun sense or in the senses that hold at least half of their
noun counts. Words for persons (WordNet's noun.person: 2,298 words before the last of the list, such as
man, sir, father, guy, girl, dad) and the words of `sources/coverage/not-things.tsv` are left out. The rule is at
the top of `tools/coverage.mjs`.

"Has a drawing" compares the word with the name of a `k.objects` object (or its plural). Every such drawing was
looked at next to the gloss of the sense kept (`sources/coverage/sense-check.tsv`): 29 drawings show another sense
of the word (the drawing `bank` is a building, the sense kept is the river bank). Such a word counts as missing
("other sense" in the list), unless an object of another name draws the sense kept (9 words). The drawing keeps its
name, since the sense it shows is a common one too.
