# What the import changed in the hand-over

Generated by `tools/import/psys2-lang-en.mjs` from PSys2 commit `78f45863` and `src/lang/en.local.mjs` at commit `447a5f9a`; do not edit by hand.

## Changed (this repository's rules)

- the temporal prepositions (`time=yes`) are a type of their own, `w.lang.en.prep.time`, so that a grammar reads them as their own category (TPREP); `next_to`, `earlier_than` are written "next to", "earlier than"
- the contraction rules "(.+)n't" -> "$1 not" became `w.lang.en.contraction.ending ending="n't" word=not`; the plural rule "consonant+y" became `suffix=y after=consonant`; `pattern=us-plural` became `exception=us-plural` (the nouns of kind us-plural-suffix and us-plural-word)
- ranges such as `value=2..5` of the ellipsis limits became `min=2 max=5`
- np.possessive-of: NP -> NP OF NP names a category twice; written NP -> NP OFNP and OFNP -> OF NP (a repeated key of a fact is a set)
- pp.time: no longer a hole: the temporal prepositions name the order in time of k.time (k.time.before: relation, first_is) in shards/hand-joins.sop
- np.all-of: the production NP -> PARTITIVE OF NP is already that of np.quant-of; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- coord.shared-suffix: the rule "VP-prefix NP [CONJ NP]+ suffix" names parts that are no categories (a prefix and a suffix of a clause); it is a construction said in words, applied by grammar.shared
- coord.adj: MOD -> MOD CONJ MOD names a category twice; written MOD -> MOD CONJMOD and CONJMOD -> CONJ MOD (a repeated key of a fact is a set)
- coord.or: NP -> NP OR NP names a category twice; written NP -> NP ORNP and ORNP -> OR NP (a repeated key of a fact is a set)
- coord.except: NP -> NP EXCEPT NP names a category twice; written NP -> NP EXCEPTNP and EXCEPTNP -> EXCEPT NP (a repeated key of a fact is a set)
- ellipsis.answer: the production S -> NP is already that of ellipsis.fragment; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- ellipsis.answer: the production S -> MOD is already that of ellipsis.fragment; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- ellipsis.answer: the production S -> NUM is already that of ellipsis.number; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- anaphora.focus: the production NP -> PRON is already that of np.pron; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- anaphora.agent: the production NP -> PRON is already that of np.pron; it is written once (the construction stays, with its reading)
- q.wh-np-aux: Q -> WH NP AUX NP names a category twice; written Q -> WH NP AUXNP and AUXNP -> AUX NP (a repeated key of a fact is a set)
- q.tag: no longer a hole: the negated auxiliaries (words of AUXNEG) and statements with be (construction s.copula) are in shards/hand-grammar.sop
- the regular expressions of the coordination parameters ("[0-9]+", "^([0-9]+|first|second|third|fourth|last)$") became `digits=yes` and repeated `word=`
- `priority` (written here, hand-core) on the productions of np.possessive-of, ellipsis.one-count, ellipsis.one-bare: each ties at the same size with another construction on its own examples, and should win
- the constructions of the question frames, joiner, correction, negation and imperative rules, and the contraction endings, carry `documentation=yes`: PSys2 wrote them to document its own readers and reads none of them as data (block 07:40 item 1); they stay, complete
- the negation pattern "(.+)n't" became `ending="n't"` and the 16 contractions without apostrophe that PSys2 also counts (dont, isnt, cant ...) as words
- the 5 frames of the act `own` of the hand-over (acts.read.own, acts.read.frame, acts.read.frame.end, acts.read.frame.shaped, acts.read.frame.form) are not read from it: the same frames are taken from src/lang/en.local.mjs, where they carry `after` (block 07:40: published, though they name no act of k.actions)
- a frame `pattern` became one `w.lang.en.part` per part, in order (`at`): `is` lead, det, verb (with `act`), word (one of `word`), phrase (a phrase group), lexicon, or role (a capture: `role`, `takes` words, rest, number, prep or lexicon, `max_words`); `optional=yes` for `?`; `quotes=yes` for `rest(quotes)`; `listed=yes` for one word written as a set (`[have]`), so that the pattern can be written again as it was; the verb groups V.set and V.apart are the verbs of the acts change and take apart of k.actions; `lit.<role>=` became `w.lang.en.fixed frame= role= value=`
- the frames and the joiner `keep` of PSys2's src/lang/en.local.mjs (commit 447a5f9a), written after the import in this world's format, are imported the same way as the hand-over's frames; the frames of the act `own` are taken from there (they carry `after`), not from the hand-over
- phrase items are written with spaces and apostrophes ("i'd like to"), not "i_d_like_to"; each phrase group has a `name`, which frames name with `phrase=`

## Left out

- `tab-only` and `tab-priority` of 6 frames written after the import: they say how the demo's tabs claim a sentence (an interface of one application)
