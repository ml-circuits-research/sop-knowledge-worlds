# usgs-water: USGS Water Science School, the water cycle

- **What:** the U.S. Geological Survey's Water Science School pages on the water cycle: the processes that move
  water (evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff and groundwater). Used for the stages of the water cycle
  in `k.time` (shard cycles-usgs).
- **Origin:** https://water.usgs.gov/edu/watercycle-kids-beg.html and
  https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/water-science-school/science/water-cycle-components (read 2026-11-15).
- **Version:** read 2026-11-15 (`core.source="usgs-water 2026-11-15"`).
- **License:** public domain: a work of the United States Government (17 U.S.C. 105; the USGS policy "Copyrights and
  Credits" treats USGS information as public domain unless a credit says otherwise); written `public-domain`.
- **Attribution:** none required. Source: U.S. Geological Survey, Water Science School.
- **How to fetch:** nothing to fetch: four stage names and a sentence each, written by hand from the pages.
- **What is kept:** the order evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff (with groundwater), and what happens at
  each, in our own words.
